Lath is a unified communications platform that brings authentication, email, and SMS together on a single contact record. Instead of stitching together a separate identity provider, email vendor, and SMS carrier — each with their own notion of who a user is — Lath gives you one API, one consent system, and one spending cap that covers everything. Every operation is available over REST, the TypeScript SDK (`@trylath/sdk`), the MCP interface, the CLI, or the dashboard.

## Three products, one contact

Every auth event, every email, and every SMS is anchored to the same contact row. There is no import job, no webhook-driven sync, and no risk of consent state drifting between systems.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for a Lath account">
    Create your account at [platform.trylath.com](https://platform.trylath.com) or via the API. No credit card is required to start. You'll receive both live and test API keys immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grab your API keys">
    Copy your secret key (`lath_live_sk…` for production, `lath_test_sk…` for development) from the dashboard. Use test keys while you build — they're safe to experiment with and won't send real messages or incur charges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first email or SMS">
    Set a monthly spending cap, then call `POST /v1/email/send` or `POST /v1/sms/send` with your contact's address and a topic. Lath checks consent, quiet hours, and the spending cap automatically before every send.
  </Step>
</Steps>
